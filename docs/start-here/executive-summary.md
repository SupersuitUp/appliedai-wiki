# Executive Summary

*The field in one page. The load-bearing claims a practitioner should know cold, each linking to its full treatment.*

---

This page is the compressed version of the wiki. It is the orientation a practitioner needs before they go deep: the handful of claims that, once internalized, change how you build, operate, and sell applied AI. Each bullet links to the page that argues it in full.

## The economic backdrop

The market AI is pouring into has a shape, and the shape is widening.

- The gap between people and firms that compound with AI and those that do not keeps widening. See [There Is No Standing Still Anymore](/perspectives/there-is-no-standing-still-anymore) and, inside organizations, [Every Company Is Quietly Sorting Its People](/perspectives/every-company-is-quietly-sorting-its-people).
- Compute is metered, and tokens are the atomic unit of AI economics. See [Tokens Are the Atomic Unit of AI Economics](/perspectives/tokens-are-the-atomic-unit-of-ai-economics) and [Cheap Tokens Are a Subsidy That Will Be Withdrawn](/perspectives/cheap-tokens-are-a-subsidy-that-will-be-withdrawn).
- Frontier gains are no longer monotonic, so treat models as swappable dependencies. See [Model Upgrades Are No Longer Strictly Upgrades](/perspectives/model-upgrades-are-no-longer-strictly-upgrades).
- Every platform that subsidized your adoption will eventually monetize it ([The Lock-In Is Coming](/perspectives/the-lock-in-is-coming)), and the answer is not to own everything. Portability is a purchase with a bill ([The Portability Tax](/concepts/the-portability-tax)); the half worth carrying is your corpus, evals, craft, and prompts ([The Moat Layer](/concepts/the-moat-layer)); and [Sovereignty](/concepts/sovereignty) is protection, which ranks below what it protects ([Sovereignty Cannot Be Sold at the Expense of Safety](/perspectives/sovereignty-cannot-be-sold-at-the-expense-of-safety)).
- Cheaper intelligence expands demand rather than shrinking it ([Jevons Paradox](/concepts/jevons-paradox)), and the clearest place to capture that today is the mid-market. See [Mid-Markets Are the AI Sweet Spot](/perspectives/mid-markets-are-the-ai-sweet-spot) and [Crossing $5M Revenue Re-Rates a Services Firm](/perspectives/crossing-5m-revenue-re-rates-a-services-firm).

## The interface shift

The chat box was the first form factor, not the final one.

- The chat is not the product. See [The Chat Is Not the Product](/perspectives/the-chat-is-not-the-product) and [You Cannot Reason About AI You Have Not Felt](/perspectives/you-cannot-reason-about-ai-you-have-not-felt).
- The hand-built GUI is becoming legacy as software gets generated on demand. See [The GUI Is Becoming Legacy](/perspectives/the-gui-is-becoming-legacy) and [Software Is Disposable, Context Is the Asset](/perspectives/software-is-disposable-context-is-the-asset).
- The unit that ships work is becoming a packaged, runnable capability, not an app. See [CLIPs](/concepts/clips).

## The strategy shift

When execution gets cheap, the leverage moves up the stack.

- Strategy becomes the scarce input as execution commoditizes. See [Strategy Is the New Execution](/perspectives/strategy-is-the-new-execution).
- The durable plan is built on what will not change. See [Strategy Starts From What Will Not Change](/perspectives/strategy-starts-from-what-will-not-change).
- Capability is uneven, so map the [Task Difficulty Does Not Predict AI Performance](/perspectives/task-difficulty-does-not-predict-ai-performance) and stage adoption in [Efficiency Wins Do Not Predict New-Category Wins](/perspectives/efficiency-wins-do-not-predict-new-category-wins).

## The operator shift

The constraint is no longer the model. It is the human in the loop.

- You are the bottleneck, and the bottleneck moves as you clear it. See [You Are the Bottleneck](/perspectives/you-are-the-bottleneck) and [Making One Layer Cheap Just Moves the Bottleneck](/perspectives/making-one-layer-cheap-just-moves-the-bottleneck).
- Volume is free, so the discipline is curation, not production. See [Great Work Is a Byproduct of Volume Plus Taste](/perspectives/great-work-is-a-byproduct-of-volume-plus-taste), [Throughput Without Taste Is a Slop Factory](/perspectives/throughput-without-taste-is-a-slop-factory), and [Agents Compress Work Onto Your Judgment](/perspectives/agents-compress-work-onto-your-judgment).
- The failure modes are human: overtrust, overconfidence, and abdication. See [Generation Is Cheap, Discrimination Is the Job](/perspectives/generation-is-cheap-discrimination-is-the-job), [AI Fluency Manufactures False Confidence](/perspectives/ai-fluency-manufactures-false-confidence), [LLMs Handle Judgment, Code Handles Everything Else](/perspectives/llms-handle-judgment-code-handles-everything-else), and [Permissions Are the Load-Bearing Layer of a Harness](/perspectives/permissions-are-the-load-bearing-layer-of-a-harness).
- Treat the model as a teammate you brief and supervise. See [Teammate Discipline](/disciplines/teammate-discipline).
