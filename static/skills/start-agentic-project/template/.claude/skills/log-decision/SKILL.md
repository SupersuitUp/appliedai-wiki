# Log a decision

Append to `LOG.md`. Never rewrite an existing entry.

Capture: what was decided, what the alternatives were, and why this one. If it
reverses an earlier decision, add a forward-pointing line to that entry rather
than editing it. The reasoning is what you come back for, and a reversed decision
with its reasoning intact is more useful than a tidy record.

Decisions only. Not activity. Activity belongs in `STATE.md` under Done.
