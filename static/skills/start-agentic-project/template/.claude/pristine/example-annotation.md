# Annotation: <source name>

Dictate this. Do not type it. It needs to be honest and long enough to be useful,
not polished. `check.py` rejects anything under 200 bytes as a placeholder.

**What is in it.** Two or three sentences on what this source actually says, in
your own words. If you cannot do this, you have not read it.

**What is useful.** The specific parts that serve your argument, and roughly
where they are so you can find them again.

**What you disagree with.** The part that is wrong, overstated, or dated. This is
the most valuable section and the one people skip: agreement is cheap.

**Which part of the deliverable this serves.** Name the claim or the section.
